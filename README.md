# Mama Digital

A mobile app for Mama Mboga's vegetable business, built with Kotlin in Android Studio.
Customers can browse fresh produce, build an order, and send it to Mama Mboga on WhatsApp.

**Course:** CSE 2414 / BIT 2222 Mobile Application Development (CAT 1)
**Student:** YOUR NAME, REGISTRATION NUMBER

## The problem
Customers do not know what Mama Mboga has in stock or at what price, and have to walk to her stall to find out. Busy customers are lost.

## The solution
Mama Digital shows her products and prices, lets customers build an order, and sends it to her on WhatsApp. It also links to her Instagram, Facebook and Twitter pages.

## Business model
- **Customers:** households, working people in nearby estates, small restaurants
- **Revenue:** direct sales, delivery fee, bundle deals (e.g. weekly vegetable box), daily offers
- **Payment:** M-Pesa and cash on delivery
- **Marketing:** her social media pages and customers sharing the app
- **Costs:** stock, transport, mobile data
- **Future improvements:** in-app M-Pesa payment, stock database, order history, customer accounts

## Features
- Splash screen
- Product grid with categories, prices and emojis
- Cart with + and - buttons and a live total
- Checkout form (name, phone, location, payment method)
- Order sent to WhatsApp with all the details
- Contact and social media screen (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, call, share)
- Recipe of the Day, which changes with the day of the week
- English and Swahili switch

## How to run
1. Clone this repository
2. Open it in Android Studio
3. Click **Sync Project with Gradle Files**
4. Run the app on an emulator or an Android phone
